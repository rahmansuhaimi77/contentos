COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you enquire.

Can I ask for a specific model?

Yes. Start with the exact model you are looking for and add any storage, colour or budget preference you already know.

Why are prices not fixed on every section?

Current price and availability can depend on the specific unit. Confirm the current option before treating a price as final.

What details should I ask about?

Model, storage, colour, condition, battery health when available, warranty or accessories when applicable, and the current price.

Can I enquire even if I am still comparing?

Yes. Tell us what you are considering and your budget so the conversation starts from the right place.
